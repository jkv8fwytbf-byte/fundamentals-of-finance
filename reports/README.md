They are not part of the reading order in docs/0-START-HERE.txt, and nothing here is needed before you say "go".

| File | Made by | Date | What it is | Caveat |

| how-indian-business-groups-work.pdf | Codex | 7 September 2026 | "How Indian Business Groups Work", a 36 page illustrated field guide with the subtitle "Ownership. Money. Control. Expansion." ## Things to know

- Nothing in this folder is investment advice. The guide explains how business groups are put together; it does not tell you what to do with any share.
- To open either file, double-click it. The PDF opens in your usual PDF reader and the HTML page opens in your browser.
- Both files are snapshots of the day they were made. Neither is updated when the plan or the documents change.
- The PDF refers to Indian law and company filings as they stood in September 2026. Check the named source before you rely on a rule.
