# Section 4: Who does what, and when {#r5-owners}

::: {.reading-only .read-first}
**Follow who does what.** Separate your learning and review work from the foundation build, then connect the timing to the milestones.
:::

**In short.** This section is the plan's calendar. [The clock starts]{.reading-highlight} on the day you say "go", not today. Only M0, the documents and diagrams, is done (plan section 5).

- Week 1, me. I will build the foundation, M2, in a private repo you own, plus a walkthrough that explains every file (plan section 4). A repo is the folder holding the code and its change history. Private means only you can open it.
- Week 1, you, 3 evenings (plan section 4). You will open the accounts, with a $75 spend limit on OpenRouter, the door through which the system rents AI models (plan section 4). You will set up Cursor, the code editor with an AI helper inside it: Privacy Mode, which stops Cursor storing your code; the MCP file, which lists the tools the helper may call; and Plan mode, where the helper plans and waits (plan section 4). You will read 2-damodaran-essentials.pdf and the first three chapters of 4-the-guide.pdf (plan section 4).
- Weeks 2 to 4, you in Cursor (plan section 4). You will learn by changing what exists: the 10-minute exercises, your first pull requests, adding companies to the watchlist, reviewing memos, and reading the dashboard (plan section 4). A pull request is a proposed change that is checked before it joins the main code. The watchlist is the list of companies the system values. A memo is the written valuation the system drafts for your approval. One evening a week you will work with the AI helper off (plan section 4). I will be on call when something blocks you.
- Weeks 4 to 8, you in Cursor, me on request (plan section 4). India through EODHD, a paid data service for Indian financials; the sector-exposure and accuracy views; evals in Langfuse, tests of the AI's answers and the tool that records them; Monte Carlo, which reruns a valuation many times to give a range; reverse DCF, which works back from today's price to the growth it assumes; opposing memos; and the market-regime panel, which describes the market's state and never tells you what to do (plan section 4).
- Later, only if others use it: a web app, commercial data licenses, and sub-agents, small AI helpers that run alone (plan section 4).

> **Comment**
> **What it means for you:** It is a relay race. I run the first leg, you run the rest with me beside the track, and nothing starts until you say go. Your week 1 is setup and reading, not coding.
> **Decision needed:** Decision 1, go or wait, after reading 1-read-this-first.pdf and 2-damodaran-essentials.pdf; decision 3, the free accounts, in M1 after go; decision 2, the first 10 to 20 US companies, in weeks 2 to 4.
> **Money:** Cursor Pro $20 per month, already paid; OpenRouter first purchase $20 of credits, $75 spend limit; EODHD $60 per month only from M4 (plan section 6).
> **When:** M0 done today, 2026-09-14; M1 you, 3 evenings, starts only when you say "go"; M2 me, days 3 to 7 after go; M3 you, weeks 2 to 4; M4 weeks 4 to 8; M5 later, only if others ever use it (plan section 5).
