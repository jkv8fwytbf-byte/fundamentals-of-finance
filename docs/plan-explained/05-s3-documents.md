# Section 3: The four documents and the diagrams

**In short.** Plan section 3 lists four documents and one set of diagrams. I deliver all of them in milestone M0, the first checkpoint of the plan (plan section 5). Each document exists as a PDF, a file that looks the same on every screen. The plan labels the documents A to D. The numbers in the file names are the order in which you should read them, so letters and numbers differ on purpose. The page counts below come from the delivered files as they sit on disk today, 2026-09-14.

- **A. Read This First** became 1-read-this-first.pdf. It explains the whole system in plain words with pictures: every part, every outside service, the accounts you will open, the correctness rules, and how to set up Cursor, your coding editor (plan section 3). The delivered file has 18 pages.
- **B. Teaching Guide** became 4-the-guide.pdf. It tells you where and how to learn each tool, in order, with hours and links, and ends with a 12-week schedule (plan section 3). The delivered file has 104 pages.
- **C. Damodaran, the important parts** became 2-damodaran-essentials.pdf. Damodaran is the professor whose valuation method this system follows. The document is your revision notes: the story-to-numbers frame, the DCF chain, country risk with the India numbers, and his rules of thumb (plan section 3). DCF means discounted cash flow, valuing a business by its future cash, reduced for time and risk. The delivered file has 53 pages.
- **D. The Other Side** became 3-the-other-side.pdf. It collects his own warnings, his misses with the numbers, the academic critiques, twelve episodes where someone bet against him and won, and what this means for our product (plan section 3). The delivered file has 47 pages.
- **FigJam diagrams.** FigJam is an online whiteboard made by Figma, a browser design tool. There are five boards: architecture, nightly data flow, the valuation chain, the memo pipeline, and the milestone map (plan section 3). I create them through the Figma connector, a link between my tools and your Figma account, so you can open and edit them (plan section 3). If your view seat blocks creation, the same diagrams ship as images inside document A, where the links also live (plan section 3).
