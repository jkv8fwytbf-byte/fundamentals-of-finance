# How to read this document

This is document 5 of the five I promised you. The first four were delivered under M0 (plan section 3): 1-read-this-first.pdf, 2-damodaran-essentials.pdf, 3-the-other-side.pdf and 4-the-guide.pdf. A PDF is a fixed-layout document file. This fifth one walks through the approved plan, section by section, with my comment on each part.

**What the plan is.** The plan is a written file, version 4, dated 2026-09-14. A milestone is a named stage of the work with a clear finish line. The plan has six milestones, M0 to M5 (plan section 5). Only M0 is approved. M0 is the documents and diagrams, and I finished it today. Everything after M0 is parked, meaning agreed in direction but frozen until you say "go". Nothing after M0 has been built: no repository work, no accounts, no installs. A repository, or repo, is the folder where code lives with its history.

**How each chapter is shaped.** Every chapter after this one has the same three parts.

- **In short.** A few plain sentences that restate that part of the plan, with every term defined on first use.
- **Comment.** An indented note with four fixed lines. *What it means for you* says what changes for you. *Decision needed* names one of your three decisions, or says none. *Money* gives the cost from the budget (plan section 6), or none. *When* gives the milestone and its calendar words (plan section 5).
- **Read more.** The PDF and chapter that go deeper.

**Reading order.** Read 1-read-this-first.pdf first. Read 2-damodaran-essentials.pdf before this document or alongside it. On your first pass, skip the chapter on Section 10. Section 10 holds the technical specifications, meaning exact descriptions of how each piece is built. One example is the tables of the database, the one store for the numbers (plan section 10.3, named in plan section 5). It will make more sense once the foundation exists.

**Your three decisions.** These are the only decisions the plan asks of you. Everything else is already decided.

1. Go or wait. Say "go" only after you have read PDF 1 and PDF 2. Nothing after M0 starts before that word (plan section 0).
2. Which 10 to 20 US companies go on the first watchlist, the list of companies the system values and re-values. I will need the first names during M2, days 3 to 7 after go, and 20 names by the end of M3, weeks 2 to 4 (plan section 5).
3. Yes or no to opening the free accounts in plan section 7: GitHub, OpenRouter, Neon, Pinecone, Voyage, Cloudflare, Langfuse and Hex. Mistral and EODHD, two paid services, come later (plan section 6). This happens in M1, three evenings, only after go (plan section 5).
