## 5. Snapshot cadence & auto-refresh

**Cadence (verbatim from `New_Home_Page/datahistory.md` and `datacurrent.md`):** "I update most of the data only once a year, in the first two weeks of January." Current corpus: *"Data of last full update: **January 9, 2026**; Country risk premiums last updated: **January 5, 2026**."* Exceptions updated intra-year: **country risk premiums** (Jan/Apr/Jul — `ctrypremApr26`, `ctrypremJuly26`) and **implied ERP** (monthly — 16 `ERP<Mon><YY>.xlsx` vintages, latest Sept 2026), plus the daily `Alldata<Month><Year>.xlsx` tape.

**To refresh automatically from `pages.stern.nyu.edu`:**
1. Base URL pattern: `https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/datasets/<name>.xls[x]`. `datacurrent.md` contains **244 distinct dataset URLs** — it is a ready-made, machine-parsable download manifest (`grep -o 'https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/datasets/[A-Za-z0-9&._-]*'`). Archived years live at `…/pc/archives/<name><YY>.xls`. Implied-ERP files at `…/pc/implprem/ERP<Mon><YY>.xlsx`.
2. Generate names as `{family} × {"", Europe, Japan, emerg, India, China, Rest, Global}` with the four hard-coded exceptions (`betas`, `pedata`, `pbvdata`, `psdata`, `DollarUS`) and the `.xls` vs `.xlsx` split.
3. Dated files (`ctryprem<Mon><YY>.xlsx`, `ERP<Mon><YY>.xlsx`) must be **discovered**, not guessed — scrape `datacurrent.html` / `impliederp.html` link lists monthly. `ctryprem.xlsx` (undated) is the canonical January file; the dated ones are the intra-year updates.
4. Schedule: full crawl **mid-January**; `ctryprem*` **quarterly (Jan/Apr/Jul)**; `ERP*` **monthly**; `Alldata*` **daily** if you want a live riskfree/ERP.
5. Change detection: every sheet carries a `Date updated:` cell in row 1 (`2026-01-05`) and a `What is this data?` + region cell in row 4 — parse those as the version stamp rather than relying on HTTP headers.
6. Parsing: `.xls` = BIFF8 (needs `xlrd<2` / `libreoffice --convert-to`); `.xlsx` = OOXML (`openpyxl`).
