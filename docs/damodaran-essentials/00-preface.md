# How to read this document

This is document C of four. Document A ("Read This First") explains the system we are building.
This one explains the thinking the system automates, in the order Aswath Damodaran teaches it.
It is not exam preparation. It is the dense version a friend would give you before you open his
lectures again, with a pointer to the packet, session or blog post behind each idea.

Read the seven chapters in file order, one evening each:

1. Why value at all: price versus value, the story-to-numbers frame, the 3P test.
2. The accounting minimum and the base year: where every input number comes from.
3. The DCF chain: what the calculator does, step by step, with the Almarai golden case.
4. Discount rates and country risk: risk-free rate, equity risk premium, betas, cost of debt, India.
5. Growth, terminal value and the dark side: where valuations go wrong and how to show a range.
6. Relative valuation and what the market prices in: multiples, the implied premium, the regime panel.
7. India and the rules of thumb: the currency, tax and data choices, and a one-page checklist.

Every number in the text has a "Source:" line under it that names the file in the local corpus
(`~/Downloads/financeMD/`) or the research report it came from. When a chapter says "the
calculator", it means the fcffsimpleginzu workbook that the engine reproduces to one part in a
million. Nothing here is advice to buy or sell anything; these are estimation methods and their
limits.

Where this document stops, document D ("The Other Side") starts: it collects the people who
disagree with him, and what he has said back.
