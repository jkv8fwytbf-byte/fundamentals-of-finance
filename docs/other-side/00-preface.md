# How to read this document

This is document D of four. It exists because a system that only ever agrees with one man is a
bad system. Damodaran himself wrote a book called "The Dark Side of Valuation", and he grades his
own misses in public; this document collects both his self-criticism and the criticism from
academics, practitioners and the investors who bet against his numbers.

Read it after document C, so that you know what is being criticized. The chapters are in file
order: a read-me on how to be fair; his own dark side in ten claims; his misses with the numbers;
the academic and practitioner critiques; twelve episodes where someone disagreed and what
happened; a reading list ordered for a beginner; and, last, the design rules the app inherits
from all of this.

Where a number, date or link appears, it comes from the critics research report gathered for this
project or from a post in the local corpus, and the "Source:" line says which. Where the corpus
has a gap (the 2012 and 2025 blog years are missing; the three Dark Side decks are image PDFs),
the text says so rather than filling it in.
